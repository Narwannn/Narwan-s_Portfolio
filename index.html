RESEARCH FOCUS

What happens when an AI model gets a question that doesn't have enough information? That was the question behind my summer research with Prof. Tian Lan's group at GW.

I used Python to build and validate 500+ quantitative reasoning problems with K=3 and K=4 branching structures. I checked the math across different branches and reviewed model answers for reasoning errors and unsupported assumptions.
