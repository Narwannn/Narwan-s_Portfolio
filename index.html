02 / MISSION LOG

RESEARCH FOCUS

Good answers
start with
better questions.

In Prof. Tian Lan's research group, I worked on ill-posed quantitative problems: questions with missing, ambiguous, or contradictory information. The goal was to examine how LLMs handle uncertainty in the problem itself.

My work included creating structured problems, checking their mathematical consistency, reviewing model outputs, and flagging issues in the dataset.
